About Cleanity

We are a global community of young people who believe that real environmental change starts at home, in our neighborhoods, and with direct action that makes a tangible difference.

Our Mission

Real change starts at home

Cleanity is a youth-driven initiative, that prides itself on direct environmental and action, through community based cleanups, education, and programming that is accessible. Cleanity is focused on reducing waste from shared spaces, changing the public's perception of responsiblity, and making participation in any environmental action something that is real, ongoing, rather than intangible and intermittent. Cleanity believes that real change starts at home. This could mean day-of events, organizing cleanups in parks or neighborhoods, creating systems and tools that would allow volunteers to manage and quantify their efforts, and creating educational opportunities for younger students, about sustainability and the effect of waste in the long run. Our work embodies reliability and experiential learning, we want to teach youth that they care about waste, the environment, and the future.

To us environmental degradation is not something that is abstract, it is present, visible, and urgent. It is something we walk past, every day. Cleanity exists in that awareness and is looking for ways to find real solutions created by the people who deal with it. We do not believe that we should rely on institutions or hierarchical organization, we believe that our grassroots work is a response to 'top down' organizations working in the community and youth can lead their own community. Our method is collaborative, and responsive, it also celebrates the idea that public spaces are worth caring for, and when participants, particularly youth, are offered all the tools and space to participate, they will effect real, tangible change.

Our Story

How we got started

💡

The Idea

Started by young people who were tired of seeing environmental problems discussed without direct action. We realized that waiting for others to fix the world wasn't working.

🤝

Community Building

We began organizing local cleanup events, connecting with like-minded youth, and building a network of people who wanted to make real, tangible change in their communities.

🚀

Global Expansion

Today, we operate in multiple cities worldwide, empowering youth to lead environmental action in their own neighborhoods and creating lasting change from the ground up.

Our Values

What drives us forward

⚡

Direct Action

We believe in taking immediate, tangible action rather than waiting for systemic change. Every cleanup, every piece of trash collected, every community educated makes a real difference.

👥

Youth Leadership

Young people have the energy, creativity, and stake in the future that make them natural environmental leaders. We empower youth to lead their communities.

🌱

Grassroots Approach

Real change happens from the bottom up. We focus on community-level solutions that can be implemented by local teams and scaled organically.

🎓

Education & Learning

We combine action with education, teaching sustainability principles and environmental awareness through hands-on experience and community engagement.

🔄

Ongoing Commitment

Environmental action isn't a one-time event. We build systems and habits that create lasting change through consistent, regular community involvement.

🌍

Global Impact

While we work locally, we think globally. Our network of communities shares knowledge, strategies, and support to amplify our collective impact.
