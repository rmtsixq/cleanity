Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How can I organize a cleanup in my city?

+

Contact us through the form above with "Organize a Cleanup" as your subject. We'll connect you with our regional coordinator who will provide you with all the resources, training, and support you need to organize a successful cleanup event.

Is there an age requirement to join Cleanity?

+

While Cleanity is youth-led, we welcome supporters of all ages! For leadership positions, we typically look for individuals aged 16-30, but volunteers can participate at any age with appropriate supervision for minors.

Do you provide cleanup supplies and equipment?

+

Yes! We provide starter kits for new cleanup organizers including gloves, trash bags, safety vests, and data collection sheets. For larger events, we can connect you with local sponsors and partners for additional supplies.

How do you track the impact of cleanup events?

+

We use our custom mobile app and data collection system to track metrics like pounds of trash collected, number of volunteers, types of waste found, and areas cleaned. This data helps us measure our global impact and improve future events.
